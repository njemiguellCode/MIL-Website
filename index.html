FREQUENT.QUESTIONS // FAQ.DB

Isn't using AI just like using a calculator?

Not really, but we can understand the logic. A calculator replaces one narrow, verifiable skill, while genAI touches judgement, writing, and reasoning, where errors are subtle and verification is the very skill you'd be skipping. Use it like a calculator: for the parts you can check.

Is all AI use overreliance?

No. Overreliance is specifically way too much trust in accepting outputs without verification regardless of stakes. Using AI to brainstorm, accelerate drafts, or explore, while retaining review and responsibility, is healthy collaboration.

Why do confident answers make us trust more?

Fancy word read as truth. Our human minds equate polished and confident delivery with competence. That is why verification, not vibes, must carry the weight.

What is the single best habit against overreliance?

Ask: “Could I explain and defend this if challenged?” If not, you are not done. One honest question, applied consistently, restores the human to the loop.
