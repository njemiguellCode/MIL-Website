WHAT IS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the capability of computational systems to perform tasks typically associated with human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and decision-making. It is a field of research in engineering, mathematics, and computer science that develops and studies methods and software that enable machines to perceive their environment and use learning and intelligence to take actions that maximise their chances of achieving defined goals.

SOURCE: WIKIPEDIA (EN.WIKIPEDIA.ORG/WIKI/ARTIFICIAL_INTELLIGENCE)

WHAT IS OVERRELIANCE ON A.I.?

Overreliance on AI is the behavioral tendency of human users to place extreme trust in the output of artificial intelligence — accepting generated content without verification and critical evaluation. It is one of the biggest effects of AI being mainstream, occurring when individuals transfer tasks to algorithms, assuming machine responses are always accurate due to the confident tone of AI outputs, which risks introducing undetected errors and threatens to break down human analytical skills.

SOURCE: STANFORD (HTTPS://HAI.STANFORD.EDU/NEWS/AI-OVERRELIANCE-PROBLEM-ARE-EXPLANATIONS-SOLUTION)

THE ISSUE

AI tools such as ChatGPT or Gemini are now everyday life for the majority of people, used for work, homework, and even personal seeking advice for personal decisions. But research shows this convenience and heavy reliance on AI is gradually weakening people's ability to think and reason for themselves, regardless of age or profession.

FACTS AND STATISTICS

  • Fact. Over-relying on AI can cause cognitive offloading, reducing a person's ability to analyze and evaluate information independently.
  • Stat. Udewark Research Institute reports that 77% of employees say AI has increased their workload or pressure, encouraging reliance on unverified AI outputs.
  • Stat. Pew Research Center found that 55% of adults believe relying on AI worsens creative thinking, with respondents accepting AI results without verification.

SOURCES: UPWORK / PEW RESEARCH CENTER

WHAT WE SHOULD ALL DO

  • Try solving the problem and answering it by yourself first.
  • Use AI for guidance as a tool, not as an answer sheet.
  • Always double-check the information AI gives.
  • Compare AI answers with multiple reliable sources.
  • Add your own ideas and understanding rather than just copy and pasting.
  • Learn the skill instead of simply copying the result AI gives.

FREQUENT.QUESTIONS // FAQ.DB

Isn't using AI just like using a calculator?

Partly — and the comparison is instructive. A calculator replaces one narrow, verifiable skill; general AI touches judgment, writing, and reasoning, where errors are subtle and verification is the very skill you'd be skipping. Use it like a calculator: for the parts you can check.

Is all AI use overreliance?

No. Overreliance is specifically uncalibrated trust — accepting outputs without verification regardless of stakes. Using AI to brainstorm, accelerate drafts, or explore, while retaining review and responsibility, is healthy collaboration.

Why do confident answers make us trust more?

Fluency reads as truth. Human heuristics equate polished, confident delivery with competence — a bias automation inherits and amplifies. That is why verification, not vibes, must carry the weight.

What is the single best habit against overreliance?

Ask: “Could I explain and defend this if challenged?” If not, you are not done. One honest question, applied consistently, restores the human to the loop.
